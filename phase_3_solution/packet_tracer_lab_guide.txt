NETSAGE AI — PACKET TRACER DEMO LAB GUIDE

Build this small demonstration in Cisco Packet Tracer and save it as:
Name-College Name-AI.pkt

Suggested topology:
PC1 --- S1 --- R1 --- S2 --- Server1
                         |
                        PC2

Suggested VLANs:
VLAN 10 USERS: 192.168.10.0/24
VLAN 20 SERVERS: 192.168.20.0/24
VLAN 30 GUEST: 192.168.30.0/24

Demo fault:
1. Put PC1 in VLAN 30 but configure R1/S1 so VLAN 30 is not allowed on the trunk.
2. Give PC1 192.168.30.10/24 and gateway 192.168.30.1.
3. Verify with:
   show vlan brief
   show interfaces trunk
   show ip interface brief
   show ip route
4. Capture the symptom and command output in the NetSage case.
5. Diagnose: missing VLAN 30 on the trunk.
6. Human reviewer marks the recommendation Accepted/Edited/Rejected.
7. After approval, correct the trunk configuration and verify with ping.
8. Save the Packet Tracer project as the required .pkt file.

Do not submit this text file as a .pkt. Open/save the topology using Cisco Packet Tracer.
